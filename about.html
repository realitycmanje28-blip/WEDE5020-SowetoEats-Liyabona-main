About Soweto Eats

We connect you to the best local food spots in Soweto. Founded in 2024 to support township entrepreneurs and bring authentic township flavors to your door.

Our mission: Support local. Eat local. Grow Soweto.

Why We Exist

Soweto Eats was created to make it easier for residents and visitors to discover delicious, affordable, and authentic food from local restaurants and home-based food businesses.

What We Celebrate

  • Local township cuisine
  • Small business growth
  • Community pride and connection
  • Convenient access to quality food
